**A helper for your Pokémon community 🎉**

Poke Helper brings the games and tools a community server needs, in a look that changes with the seasons.

**🎮 Fun**

• **Counting:** count up together, with lives, math like `6*7`, records and a leaderboard

• **Leveling:** XP for chatting and voice, rank cards, weekly and monthly leaderboards and level-up roles

• **Pokédex:** look up any Pokémon with its types, stats and evolutions, shiny too

• **Who's that Pokémon?** Guess a Pokémon from its silhouette, with hints

• **Friend codes:** everyone's Switch and Pokémon game codes in one place, with a Copy button for phones

• **Starboard:** messages with enough reactions are collected in their own channel

• **Giveaways:** with rules for who can enter, bonus entries for roles, a list of who's in, and winners who claim their prize in time, or it goes to someone new

**🛠️ Tools**

• **Moderation:** warn, time out, kick and ban, also for a while, with a DM telling them why, and everyone's case history to sort, change and delete, all also from the menu on someone's name

• **Server logs:** edited and deleted messages, members, moderation, roles, channels, voice and giveaways, with who did it and a Copy IDs button that works on phones

• **Temporary voice channels:** join to get your own channel, lock it, hide it, set a limit and pick moderators

• **Birthdays:** announcements and a birthday role, in everyone's own timezone

• **Role menus:** members pick their own roles with a button

• **Sticky messages:** keep a message, like a channel's rules, at the bottom of the chat

• **Reminders:** in a few minutes or at a date and time, once or repeating, by DM or in the channel

**✨ Easy to use**

• Settings panels with buttons instead of a long list of commands

• Every feature can be turned off without losing its settings

• English and German, following each member's Discord language

• `/privacy` to download or delete your data at any time
